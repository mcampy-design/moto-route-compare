How to export Timeline from Google Maps
  1. In the Google Maps app, tap your profile picture, then Your Timeline.
  2. Tap the three dots (or the gear), then Timeline settings.
  3. Scroll down to Export Timeline data and save the .json file.
  4. Come back here and choose that file. It holds your whole history, so one export covers every ride until you export again.

Your Timeline file is read in your browser and never uploaded. When you build a GPX, that day's logged points (not the rest of your history) are sent to the free public OpenStreetMap routing service at router.project-osrm.org so they can be matched to roads. Timeline only logs a point every minute or so, so tight curves are approximated and a few sections may follow a parallel road. Sections that can't be matched confidently keep the original logged points.
